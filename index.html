    Premium Pass

    A new season starts every week with a new color scheme and map decorations. Everything you unlock stays yours. Earn XP: 50 per elimination, 15 bonus for a headshot finish, 80 for finishing a match, 150 more for a win, 30 per Holdout wave, plus daily challenges on the Play tab. Season 1 has 50 tiers of 1,000 XP; from Season 2 every season has 75 tiers of 599 XP, with coins every 5 tiers. Premium pass adds +25% XP. Progress saves on this device and travels with your backup code.

    🛡️ Clan Pick a 2-4 letter clan tag. It shows next to your name and puts your eliminations on the Clans leaderboard. Friends who use the same tag are in your clan.
