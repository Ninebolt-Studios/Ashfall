Get rid of the Apple apps you never use.

Declutter for Mac

Pick which apps to remove from a checklist and Declutter moves them to the Trash. It shows the size of each app and only lists what's actually on your Mac.

Download Declutter for Mac
Version 1.0 · 80 KB · macOS 10.15 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

What it can remove

  • Pages
  • Numbers
  • Keynote
  • iMovie
  • GarageBand
  • GarageBand sound library (often several GB)

The GarageBand sound library is only offered when Logic Pro and MainStage aren't installed, because they share it.

First time opening it

Declutter isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time:

  1. Open Declutter.zip in Downloads and drag Declutter into Applications.
  2. Double-click it. If macOS says it can't be opened, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway and confirm.
  4. If you're asked to let Declutter control Finder, click Allow. Finder does the moving, which is what lets you use Put Back later.

Changed your mind?

Until you empty the Trash, right-click the app in the Trash and choose Put Back. After that, all of these apps can be downloaded again for free from the App Store.

Why Safari, Mail and the rest aren't on the list: Safari, Mail, Music, TV, Photos, Messages, FaceTime, Maps, News, Podcasts, Books, Stocks, Home, Freeform, Notes and Calendar are part of macOS itself. They live on a sealed, read-only part of the disk. Removing them means turning off System Integrity Protection, which makes your Mac less secure and can break updates, so Declutter won't do it. You can still make another browser your default in System Settings → Desktop & Dock → Default web browser and take Safari off your Dock.

iPhone & iPad

Apple doesn't let any app delete other apps on iPhone or iPad, so there's nothing to download here. You don't need one: most Apple apps can be deleted by hand, and the rest can be hidden.

Delete an Apple app

  1. Touch and hold the app's icon on the Home Screen.
  2. Tap Remove App, then Delete App.

Works for these, among others:

  • Pages
  • Numbers
  • Keynote
  • iMovie
  • GarageBand
  • Clips
  • Books
  • Podcasts
  • Stocks
  • News
  • Tips
  • Home
  • Freeform
  • Journal
  • Weather
  • Translate
  • Measure
  • Voice Memos

Hide Safari (it can't be deleted)

  1. Open Settings → Screen Time. Turn Screen Time on if it isn't.
  2. Tap Content & Privacy Restrictions and turn it on.
  3. Tap Allowed Apps & Features and turn off Safari.

Safari disappears from the Home Screen. Turn the switch back on to bring it back. In the EU, Safari can be deleted like any other app.

Can't be deleted

  • Settings
  • Phone
  • Messages
  • Photos
  • Camera
  • App Store
  • Wallet
  • Health
  • Find My
On a school or work iPad, the organization may stop you from deleting or hiding apps. Ask them if the options above are greyed out.
